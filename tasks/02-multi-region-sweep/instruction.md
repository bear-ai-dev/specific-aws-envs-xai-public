Discover every region enabled for the account and sweep block-storage inventory across all of them. Return an enabled readable region even when it contains nothing, but omit a region that permanently refuses reads and let no bad region collapse the rest. Retry transient rate limits instead of treating them as unreadable, and ignore regions the account never enabled. This task includes a local AWS-compatible emulator at the preconfigured AWS_ENDPOINT_URL. Its credentials and region are already in the shell, repository AWS clients use it, no real AWS account is involved, and you may inspect its resources while working.
