A business can describe what it sells but cannot take on anyone who buys it: nothing accepts a new customer, so none is recorded against the business, no contract is opened on the offering they signed up to, and nothing downstream ever hears they exist. Onboarding one should turn away a caller whose plan has no room left for another customer, or an identifier that business is already using, but otherwise settle on an identifier and record the customer under it. A customer who pays by card needs one created on the business' own connected payment account, with the portal link handed back, unless the caller already brought one; a business that never connected its account cannot take card customers at all. A refusal leaves nothing behind. Enrolment, the opening balance, any free trial, metering the onboarding and announcing the new customer all follow from what was agreed. There is no route out of this box; the emulator on AWS_ENDPOINT_URL archives last month's provisioning records.
